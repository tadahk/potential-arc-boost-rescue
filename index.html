arc boost rescue

arc dies dec 22 and says boosts can't be exported. they're just files on your mac though, so this grabs them.

  1. grab your boosts folder. it's hidden by default, so:

    • copy this: ~/Library/Application Support/Arc/boosts
    • click the box below. a window pops up.
    • press command + shift + G together. a little "go to folder" bar appears.
    • paste, hit return, then click upload (it doesn't upload anything, chrome just calls it that).

    or: in finder, top menu go › go to folder…, paste, then drag the boosts folder onto the box.

    • put them in your new browser (don't unzip anything):

      • install violentmonkey: chrome / brave / edge / dia or firefox / zen
      • click the violentmonkey icon (it might be inside the puzzle-piece menu) › open dashboard
      • settings on the left › import from zip › pick the zip you just downloaded
      • done. your boosts work on their sites again.
      other ways
      • stylus (css only): unzip, then stylus › manage › import › stylus-import.json
      • no add-on, chrome-type browsers: unzip, chrome://extensions › developer mode on › load unpacked › chromium-extension folder
      • safari: unzip, install the free userscripts app, add the files from userscripts
